# ============================================================
# VLM-DF - per-type criteria blocks (filled into df_core.txt at {{TASK_BLOCK}}).
#
# Each block has three fixed sections:
#   D - SYMPTOMS TO REMOVE   what the D axis counts; nothing else counts
#   D - NOT A SYMPTOM        things most easily mistaken for degradation for this
#                            type (removing them is an F violation)
#   F - PROTECTED (T*)       type-specific protected properties, numbered T1/T2/...
#                            the universal P1-P6 items live in the core
#   F - LICENSED CHANGES     large changes that are LEGITIMATE for this type.
#                            Without this section the model penalises correct
#                            dehazing or correct low-light lifting as content edits.
#
# The type numbering T1..Tn restarts in every block. When several types are
# combined the ids get a type prefix (HAZE.T1) - see harness/dfscore.py.
#
# COLOUR RULES DIFFER BY TYPE: haze and lowlight license a colour change;
# rain / snow / mix do not.
#
# Each block is introduced by a delimited marker line naming its type; the
# parser keys on that delimiter, so do not reformat it.
# ============================================================
### [[haze]] ###
TASK: haze removal.

D — SYMPTOMS TO REMOVE
  the atmospheric haze/fog that reduces visibility; the whitish or greyish
  airlight film lying over objects that are visible (check buildings, roads,
  edges and textures specifically — bulk haze gone but a milky film left on
  visible content is residue); mid-ground and background content that the haze
  made unreadable staying unreadable.

D — NOT A SYMPTOM
  the sky itself. Distance blur that is genuine aerial perspective at the far
  horizon, where no content is identifiable.

F — PROTECTED (type-specific)
  T1  OBSCURED SKY UNCHANGED: if the sky is obscured by haze in Picture 1, it
      stays obscured. Turning it into a clear blue sky, or into a sky with
      fabricated clouds, is a fabrication — score F at 30 or below.
  T2  No impossible physics introduced: no unnaturally black shadows, no colour
      banding in the sky, no dark halos around edges, no localised
      "punched-through" patches.

F — LICENSED CHANGES (never a violation here)
  Global colour, contrast and white balance MAY change — the haze was a bright
  white veil, so removing it legitimately shifts colour and usually makes the
  result DARKER overall. Darker-with-restored-tonal-range is correct. Recovering
  mid-ground and background structure that the haze was hiding is the task
  itself, not invented content.


### [[lowlight]] ###
TASK: low-light enhancement.

D — SYMPTOMS TO REMOVE
  underexposure — dark regions whose content cannot be read; detail that is
  present in Picture 1 but buried in shadow; high-ISO noise and colour blotches
  concentrated in the dark areas; weak local contrast in those areas.

D — NOT A SYMPTOM
  the night-time look itself. Deliberately dark areas that carry no information
  at all (see T2).

F — PROTECTED (type-specific)
  T1  NIGHT ATMOSPHERE PRESERVED: the scene must still read as night or dusk.
      Converting it into a daylight-looking image is a violation — score F at 30 or below.
  T2  No hallucination in the darkest regions: areas carrying genuinely no
      information in Picture 1 are left plausibly dark, not filled with invented
      detail.
  T3  HUE CATEGORY PRESERVED: red objects stay red, yellow lights stay yellow.
      A hue-category shift is a violation.
  T4  Highlights preserved: light sources and bright areas are not blown out or
      clipped away by the enhancement.

F — LICENSED CHANGES (never a violation here)
  Global brightness and saturation depth MAY rise substantially — that is the
  task. Strong enhancement is expected, not over-reach.


### [[rain]] ###
TASK: rainy-scene restoration.

D — SYMPTOMS TO REMOVE
  visible rain streaks; raindrop artifacts on the lens or on the scene; the rain
  haze veil that reduced contrast; loss of background clarity caused by the rain
  (background left blurred is residue — de-streaking alone is not enough).

D — NOT A SYMPTOM
  wetness: puddles, wet pavement, wet reflections, water on surfaces. Those are
  scene content. Overcast lighting is content.

F — PROTECTED (type-specific)
  T1  RAINY AMBIANCE PRESERVED: the scene still reads as a rainy, wet scene with
      its lighting mood. Converting it into a dry, sunny scene is a violation —
      score F at 30 or below.
  T2  COLOUR AND TONE UNCHANGED: no hue shift, no saturation shift, no
      white-balance change, no recolouring. A global colour/tone change IS a
      violation for this type.
  T3  No ghosting or smearing where rain was removed; fine edges not
      over-smoothed away in the process.

F — LICENSED CHANGES (never a violation here)
  Local contrast and clarity recovery where the rain veil was obscuring content.


### [[snow]] ###
TASK: snowy-scene restoration.

D — SYMPTOMS TO REMOVE
  airborne snowflakes and snow particles occluding the view; the haze-like snow
  veil that flattened contrast.

D — NOT A SYMPTOM
  SETTLED SNOW. Snow lying on the ground, on roofs, on branches, on vehicles or
  on any surface is scene content, not an artifact. See T1.

F — PROTECTED (type-specific)
  T1  SETTLED SNOW IS CONTENT: snow resting on any surface must remain exactly as
      it is. Clearing it away is a content violation — score F at 30 or below.
  T2  COLOUR AND TONE UNCHANGED: no hue shift, no white-balance shift, no
      recolouring. A global colour change IS a violation for this type.
  T3  Overall brightness character consistent with Picture 1 — clarity may
      improve, but the mood is not restyled.
  T4  No hallucination of what was hidden behind the flakes: uncovered areas are
      reconstructed only where evidence supports it.

F — LICENSED CHANGES (never a violation here)
  Contrast and clarity recovery in the regions the veil was covering.


### [[mix]] ###
TASK: mixed realistic degradation — the general-purpose case (noise /
compression / blur, plus exposure and lighting artifacts).

D — SYMPTOMS TO REMOVE
  sensor and read noise, and colour blotches; compression artifacts — blocking,
  ringing, mosquito noise around high-contrast edges; the softness the CAPTURE
  introduced — defocus of the intended subject, camera shake, general mush;
  blown-out highlights where recoverable detail is buried; veiling glare and
  specular reflections on glass or water that obscure content.

D — NOT A SYMPTOM
  SHALLOW DEPTH OF FIELD. A background left soft by the lens aperture is a
  property of the photograph; leaving it soft is correct. Do not count bokeh as
  residual blur. Any colour cast in Picture 1 is also not a symptom — see T1.

F — PROTECTED (type-specific)
  T1  COLOUR CAST NOT CORRECTED: any colour cast in Picture 1 remains, at the
      same strength. A cast may be intentional, so correcting it is out of scope
      and IS a violation for this type.
  T2  No stylised recolouring, no hue-category shift, no white-balance change.
  T3  Bokeh preserved: an out-of-focus background is not sharpened into focus.
  T4  No hallucination in uncertain regions: heavily damaged areas are left
      plausibly unresolved rather than invented.

F — LICENSED CHANGES (never a violation here)
  Denoising, deblocking, deblurring of capture blur, and local contrast recovery.
